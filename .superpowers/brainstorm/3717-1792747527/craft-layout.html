A. Recipe book (recommended)

A grid of recipe icons. Ones you can't make yet are grey. Pick one and its recipe shows as icons: ingredients ➤ result. Press Z to craft. Easiest with arrows, D-pad and touch.

A 3×3 grid and your inventory. You move items into the grid one by one, and the result shows up when they make a recipe. The most like Minecraft and fun to discover, but slow with arrow keys and touch, and you have to know the recipes.

C. Hybrid: tabs + auto-filled grid

Recipes in tabs (TOOLS / ITEMS / BUILD). Picking one fills a Minecraft 3×3 grid for you, items slide in, and the result glows. It has the Minecraft look but is still one button to craft. It's a bit more to build.
